# MClash 1.3.6

This patch improves MClash across compact windows, accessibility, and
localized operational states.

## Responsive interface

- Reflows Connections, App Routing, Overview, the menu bar, and related sheets
  for compact widths and longer localized text.
- Keeps important history and routing details available through clearer compact
  layouts, menus, and contextual actions.

## Accessibility and interaction

- Keeps validation actions reachable, focuses the first invalid field, and
  announces save errors to VoiceOver.
- Adds keyboard, default-action, context-menu, label, and hint support across
  tables, settings, and routing controls.

## Localization

- Localizes dynamic status messages, counts, relative dates, and provider
  errors across all eight supported languages.
- Keeps longer translations readable across operational and configuration
  views.
